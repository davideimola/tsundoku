# The MCP runs verbs directly and creates entities only through the Inbox

The MCP server **executes verbs directly** against entities that already exist — record a
Reading, set a Rating, mark a Volume owned, open a Wish on a known Volume — but may only
**propose** the creation of a Story, Volume or Series, as an **Inbox** entry the owner
approves.

The risk is not in the verbs: they are narrow, reversible, and wrong-in-an-obvious-way. It
is in entity creation, where a hallucinated title, a fabricated edition or a duplicate
becomes a permanent fact the owner carries forever. Splitting there keeps the friction
where it earns its place and leaves the highest-value flow — *"finished volume 23, I'd give
it an 8"*, said out loud — fluid. The Inbox is the owner's own design: the spreadsheets
already sketch it, with the example row *"Ho comprato Ultimate Spider-Man Omnibus 1"* and
an `Azione: Sposta in Collezione` column.
